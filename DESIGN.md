# Trace

A calm workbench for engineering follow-through. The meeting is the unit of work; evidence stays next to each output.

- Colors: paper #ffffff, canvas #f7f8fb, ink #20232f, secondary #777b8a, iris #6554c0, evidence #2e8970.
- Type: DM Sans for the interface, native monospace only for code and transcript timestamps.
- Layout: quiet 224px sidebar, spacious left-aligned workspace, meeting rows with real metadata, an evidence rail in meeting review. On mobile the navigation collapses and transcript follows the draft.
- Signature: an iris meeting waveform ties the source conversation to its outputs. Restrained color identifies decisions, tasks and evidence rather than decorating every section.
- Review: avoid a generic metrics dashboard. Put the latest meeting and the next review action first; use summary numbers as compact context. No stock photos or decorative charts.
